Privacy Policy — Fell Clips
Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the Meta app "Fell Clips Publisher" and the Fell Clips clipping tool it belongs to.

What this app is
Fell Clips is a private, self-hosted tool that its owner runs on their own computer. It turns moments from public livestreams into short vertical clips, as part of clipping programmes that the streamers themselves run, and publishes those clips to the owner's own accounts: a Facebook Page, an Instagram professional account, a TikTok account and a YouTube channel.

It is not a product offered to other people. It has no users other than the owner, no sign-up, no login for anyone else, and no public interface. Nobody else can authenticate to it or send data to it.

Personal data we collect
We do not collect, store, or sell personal data about anyone.

The app reads no audience data from Facebook or Instagram. It requests no information about the people who view, like, follow or comment on the published clips, and it reads no insights about individuals and no contact information.

How the tool handles livestream content
Stream recordings. The tool records public livestreams for a short time so that a clip can be cut. These recordings stay on the owner's computer and are deleted automatically after a few hours. The only exception is footage the owner has marked to re-edit a specific clip.
Public chat. To find exciting moments, the tool counts how many public chat messages arrive per minute. It keeps only those counts. It does not store chat usernames or message text.
Published clips show the streamer as they appeared on their own public broadcast. Clips are made for the streamers' own clipping programmes and credit the streamer. A streamer who wants a clip removed can contact us at the address below, and we will take it down.
Data the app does handle
Only the owner's own credentials and content, all held locally on the owner's computer:

Access tokens for the owner's own Meta, Google/YouTube and Telegram accounts. They are stored in a local environment file and sent only to the corresponding platform's own API.
Clip files, captions, titles and post text that the tool generates and publishes to the owner's own accounts.
A local publishing log recording which clips were posted where, so the same clip is never posted twice.
None of this is shared with anyone, sold, rented, or used for advertising or profiling.

Third-party services
To make and publish a clip, the owner's own material is sent to these providers under their own terms and privacy policies:

Anthropic (Claude API): suggests clip titles, rates which clips are worth posting, and checks the video layout, using the clip's transcript and frames.
Telegram: sends clip previews to the owner's own phone for approval.
Meta (Facebook and Instagram Graph API): publishes to the owner's Page and Instagram account.
Google (YouTube Data API): publishes to the owner's YouTube channel.
TikTok: the owner posts there through TikTok's own website.
Twitch, Kick and YouTube: the public livestreams and public chat the clips come from.
Speech-to-text for captions runs locally on the owner's computer and is not sent to any service.

Cookies and tracking
The app is not a website and serves nothing to the public. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Data retention and deletion
Stream recordings are deleted automatically within hours. Clips and the publishing log stay on the owner's computer for as long as the owner keeps them, and are deleted by deleting those files. Access tokens are revoked from the owner's Meta, Google and Telegram account settings.

To ask for deletion, whether of a clip you appear in or of any data you believe this app holds about you, email the address below. We will reply within 30 days and delete anything we find. Because the app stores no personal data about other people, there is normally nothing to export.
